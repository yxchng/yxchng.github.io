Yong Xien Chng

PhD student · Tsinghua University

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, advised by Gao Huang. I build multimodal models that reason through language, interact with the world, and revise their own outputs.

My research asks how models can learn to spend more computation thinking—by searching, sketching, or refining their hidden states. Lately, I'm exploring reasoning inside a model's own forward pass, starting with looped diffusion transformers.

Looped Diffusion Transformer

Yong Xien Chng, et al.

arXiv preprint, 2026 (under review)

arXiv sooncode soon

Rerun the middle blocks of a diffusion transformer inside every denoising step. A 260M model beats models 6.5× larger at 1/5 the compute, and later loops correct mistakes made by earlier ones.

InterSketch: An Interleaved Reasoning Model with Self-correcting Visual Sketch and Stepwise Reward

Zhiwei Ning, Wenwen Tong, Xiangli Kong, Shengnan Ma, Yong Xien Chng, et al.

arXiv preprint, 2026

arXiv

A vision-language model that thinks by drawing. It sketches intermediate figures with tools, reflects on them, and is trained with stepwise rewards so RL can reach the end of long trajectories.

Aligning Vision-Language Model with Fine-Grained Semantics for Open-Vocabulary Segmentation

Yong Xien Chng, Xuchong Qiu, Yizeng Han, Kai Ding, Wan Ding, Gao Huang

IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for Video Technology, 2026

arXiv

The bottleneck in open-vocabulary segmentation is classifying masks, not finding them. Semantic-guided mask attention and tuning only the query projection fix it cheaply.

SenseNova-MARS: Empowering Multimodal Agentic Reasoning and Search via Reinforcement Learning

Yong Xien Chng*, Tao Hu*, Wenwen Tong*, Xueheng Li, Jiandong Chen, Haojia Yu, Jiefan Lu, Hewei Guo, Hanming Deng, Chengjun Xie, Gao Huang, Dahua Lin, Lewei Lu

arXiv preprint, 2025

arXivcodemodels

A VLM that searches the web, crops the image and reasons, all in one interleaved loop learned with RL. The 32B model beats Gemini-3-Pro and GPT-5.2 on multimodal search, and we release HR-MMSearch, a high-resolution search benchmark.
